The Best TH11 Base Layouts for 2026: War, Farming & Trophy Strategies
If you've just upgraded to Town Hall 11, you already know the stakes are higher. The th11 base meta in 2026 has evolved significantly — Electro Dragons, Grand Warden combos, and Queen Walks dominate the attack scene. Your base needs to counter all of it. This guide breaks down exactly what makes a strong TH11 layout, whether you're focused on Clan War, farming, or pushing trophies.

What Makes a Strong TH11 Base?
Core Design Principles
A well-designed th11 base in 2026 isn't just about protecting your Town Hall. It's about forcing attackers into bad decisions. The best layouts share three traits: compartmentalization, centralized Eagle Artillery placement, and anti-funnel design.
Compartmentalization means your base is divided into multiple sections using walls. When attackers breach one compartment, their troops get funneled into a kill zone — ideally where your Inferno Towers and X-Bows are waiting. Avoid open bases where troops can walk straight to the core.
The Eagle Artillery is TH11's most powerful defense. Always place it deep in the core, surrounded by at least two layers of compartments. If attackers can reach it with a small Queen Walk, your base is already compromised.
Defensive Building Placement
Spread your Air Defenses evenly across the base — never cluster them. Three Air Defenses in one corner means an Electro Dragon attack can take them all out with one path. Diagonal placement across the base forces air attacks to cover maximum distance, buying your other defenses more time.
Scattershots should cover as much of the base as possible. Place them on opposite sides so their splash damage zones overlap in the middle — this punishes any ground attack trying to push through the center.

TH11 War Base Strategy: Stopping 3-Stars
Anti-Electro Dragon Setup
Electro Dragon attacks are the biggest threat at TH11 in Clan War. To stop them, your Air Defenses need to be the last buildings destroyed, not the first. Surround each Air Defense with low-hitpoint buildings like Barracks and Army Camps — these act as shields, slowing the Electro Dragons' path.
Use an asymmetrical layout. Symmetrical bases are easy to funnel — attackers know exactly where everything is. An asymmetrical th11 base forces attackers to spend more time planning, and in war, that means more mistakes.
Keep your Clan Castle central and filled with a Valkyrie or Witch. When triggered, these defensive troops pull attacking ground units into your base's core where defenses are strongest.
Anti-Queen Walk Defense
Queen Walks are used to create funnels before the main attack. To counter this, place high-damage single-target Inferno Towers on the edges of your base where a Queen Walk typically starts. A Queen facing an Inferno Tower on single-target mode will burn through her hitpoints fast, even with a Healer behind her.
Add a "bait" compartment on each side of the base — a small section with low-value buildings that looks like an easy entry point. Inside, place a Spring Trap and two Giant Bombs. Attackers who take the bait waste troops and time.

TH11 Farming Base: Protecting Your Resources
Dark Elixir Storage Placement
At TH11, Dark Elixir is your most valuable resource. Protecting it means placing your Dark Elixir Storage in the absolute center of the base, deeper than your Town Hall if possible. Attackers who prioritize Dark Elixir have to commit fully — and a full commitment means they won't have enough troops to also three-star you.
Surround the Dark Elixir Storage with your two Inferno Towers and the Eagle Artillery. This cluster of high-damage defenses makes it almost impossible to reach without a full army. Most farmers will move on to easier targets.
Gold and Elixir Collector Layout
Spread your Gold Storages and Elixir Storages around the base in separate compartments. Never place two Storages in the same compartment — a single Hog Rider attack can loot both if they're adjacent.
A useful trick for farming bases: place your Town Hall slightly outside the core. This lets shield-hungry attackers grab it easily, triggering a shield for you while your actual resources remain protected inside. You lose a little trophy count but gain hours of farming time without interruption.

5 Recommended TH11 Base Layout Styles
1. The Anti-3-Star War Base
A tightly compartmentalized layout with the Eagle Artillery and both Inferno Towers forming a triangle in the core. Air Defenses are placed at maximum distance from each other. Clan Castle is central. This layout consistently forces two-star results against even experienced attackers.
2. The Ring Base
Town Hall sits in the very center, surrounded by three rings of defenses and walls. The outer ring absorbs ground spam attacks. The middle ring handles Hog Riders and Miners. The inner ring is a kill zone with both Infernos on single-target mode. Weak against air attacks, so pair with strong Air Defense placement.
3. The Anti-Electro Dragon Spread
All buildings are spread across the entire map width. Electro Dragons lose chain lightning efficiency when targets are far apart. This layout sacrifices some ground defense but completely neuters Electro Dragon attacks — ideal if your war opponents rely heavily on air.
4. The Hybrid Farming Base
Equal focus on protecting Dark Elixir and maintaining a decent trophy count. Town Hall is semi-exposed to bait attackers into a shield trigger, while all three Storages are centralized behind double-layered walls. Works well for players who farm actively during war season.
5. The Funnel Trap Base
Designed to look like an easy three-star but loaded with traps. Open-looking corridors lead troops directly into Giant Bomb clusters and Spring Trap chains. Both Inferno Towers are hidden behind what appear to be weak wall sections. Experienced attackers may see through it, but it destroys rushed or lazy attacks completely.

Common TH11 Base Mistakes to Avoid
Clustering Air Defenses in one area is the single most common mistake at TH11. It makes you an easy target for any air-heavy attack. Spread them out, even if it means they're less protected individually.
Leaving the Scattershot exposed on the edge of the base is another frequent error. Scattershots have high hitpoints but deal enormous damage — attackers will always target them first if they're reachable. Push them inward.
Ignoring trap placement is a mistake that costs players three-stars constantly. Traps are free, reset after every attack, and can completely ruin an otherwise solid push. Update your Giant Bomb and Spring Trap positions every few weeks as the meta shifts.
Finally, never copy a base layout without understanding why it works. A th11 base that performed well six months ago may already be outdated. The 2026 meta moves fast — check for layout updates regularly and adjust when you start seeing repeated three-star losses.
