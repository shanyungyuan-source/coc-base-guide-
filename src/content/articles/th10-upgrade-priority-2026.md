## TH10 Upgrade Overview

Town Hall 10 introduces the Inferno Tower, the second X-Bow, and the Grand Warden. It's one of the most significant Town Hall upgrades in the game. The problem: everything is expensive and there's a lot to build.

Following the wrong upgrade order at TH10 means spending weeks at a power level that's weak in both attack and defense. This guide gives the optimal path based on the 2026 meta.

---

## Rule #1: Never Rush TH10

Before anything else — don't rush to TH11. TH10 has unique advantages:
- You face TH10 opponents in Clan War matchmaking
- The Inferno Tower and second X-Bow make you genuinely competitive in defense
- Grand Warden upgrades are Elixir-based (cheap compared to Dark Elixir)

Rushing past TH10 with un-upgraded defenses and low heroes means entering TH11 with a base that can't hold against TH11 opponents. Spend the time at TH10 properly.

---

## Priority 1: New Buildings First

When you first arrive at TH10, immediately build:
- **Inferno Tower × 2** — Start these immediately. They are your biggest defensive upgrade and unlock new attack strategies for clanmates.
- **Grand Warden** — Build him and start upgrading. He charges with Elixir, so upgrading him doesn't compete with Dark Elixir resources.
- **Second X-Bow** — Build and upgrade alongside the Infernos.

These buildings don't exist at TH9. Until they're built, your base is incomplete.

---

## Priority 2: Heroes

This is the most impactful use of your Dark Elixir at TH10.

**Archer Queen to level 40-45** — The Queen is the most valuable upgrade at TH10. A Queen Walk at level 40+ clears an entire side of a TH10 base reliably. This unlocks 3-star attacks that are impossible with a lower-level Queen.

**Grand Warden to level 20+** — Level 20 Warden provides a meaningful Eternal Tome radius. Keep him upgrading constantly with Elixir.

**Barbarian King to level 35-40** — Important but secondary to the Queen. Keep the King upgrading but don't pause the Queen to rush the King.

Target ratios by TH10 end: Queen 45 / Warden 30 / King 40.

---

## Priority 3: Defenses

Once new buildings are built and heroes are actively upgrading, focus defenses in this order:

### Tier 1 — Upgrade Immediately
1. **Inferno Towers** (both) — These are your most impactful defensive buildings. Level 3 Infernos are a major milestone that makes your base genuinely difficult to 3-star.
2. **X-Bows** (both) — Level 4 X-Bows at TH10 are the strongest ground defense in the game at this level.

### Tier 2 — High Priority
3. **Air Defenses × 4** — Electro Dragon attacks dominate TH10 war. Max your Air Defenses before attackers start reliably 3-starring your air defense.
4. **Eagle Artillery** — The Eagle Artillery activates early in attacks (after 100 troops deployed) and deals continuous damage throughout. Getting it to level 2-3 dramatically increases its damage output.

### Tier 3 — Secondary Priority
5. **Scattershot × 1** (TH10 has one) — Strong splash damage. Upgrade after Tier 1 and 2.
6. **Wizard Towers** — Splash damage is valuable but less urgent than the unique defenses above.
7. **Cannons and Archer Towers** — These upgrade significantly at TH10 but are lower priority than the big-ticket items.

---

## Priority 4: Walls

Walls at TH10 can reach level 11 (dark gray) and level 12 (blackish). The HP jump is significant, especially for the core compartments.

**Strategy:** Don't upgrade all walls at once. Prioritize walls around your:
1. Dark Elixir Storage
2. Inferno Towers and X-Bows
3. Eagle Artillery
4. Core compartment containing the Town Hall

Outer walls can wait — losing outer buildings doesn't lose wars. Losing the core does.

---

## Priority 5: Troops and Spells

Troop upgrades directly impact your attack performance. Focus on:

**Attack-critical:**
- **Lava Hound** — Enables LaLoon, the most consistent TH10 attack
- **Electro Dragon** — Dominant air troop; high priority
- **Hog Rider** — Used in multiple strategies; upgrade continuously

**Support:**
- **Balloon** — Pairs with Lava Hound; keep upgraded
- **Healer** — Essential for Queen Walks
- **Earthquake Spell** — Used in nearly every TH10 strategy

**Deprioritize:**
- Troops you don't use in war (Golems if you're running air attacks, etc.)
- Max out attack troops before army camp capacity troops

## The TH10 Upgrade Checklist

Use this as a weekly reference:

**Always active:**
- [ ] Both heroes upgrading (Queen and Warden simultaneously when Elixir allows)
- [ ] At least one Builder on Inferno Towers until maxed
- [ ] At least one Builder on X-Bows until maxed

**Monthly milestones:**
- [ ] Month 1-2: Both Infernos to level 3, Grand Warden built and to level 10
- [ ] Month 2-3: Both X-Bows maxed, Queen to level 40
- [ ] Month 3-4: Air Defenses maxed, Eagle Artillery to level 3
- [ ] Month 4-6: Queen to level 45, Warden to level 25, remaining defenses maxed

## Common TH10 Upgrade Mistakes

**Upgrading Cannons and Archer Towers before Infernos.** Cannons and Archer Towers are familiar from earlier TH levels — but Inferno Towers are uniquely powerful at TH10. Always prioritize the new buildings.

**Neglecting the Warden because he uses Elixir.** Players focus all resources on Dark Elixir buildings and let the Warden sit idle. He upgrades with Elixir — there's no reason not to keep him upgrading at all times.

**Maxing walls before defenses.** Full level-12 walls with level-1 Infernos is far weaker than level-9 walls with maxed Infernos. Defenses first, walls after.

**Leaving Air Defenses at level 6.** At TH10, Air Defenses upgrade to level 7 — a significant HP and damage boost. Electro Dragon attacks shred bases with under-leveled Air Defenses.
