## Welcome to Clash of Clans

Clash of Clans is a mobile strategy game where you build a village, train an army, and battle other players. The game has been running since 2012 and is continuously updated — it's deeper than it looks, and the learning curve can be steep without a guide.

This guide gives new players the foundational knowledge to progress efficiently and avoid the most common beginner traps.

---

## The Core Loop

Every session in COC follows the same loop:

1. **Collect resources** from Gold Mines, Elixir Collectors, and Dark Elixir Drills
2. **Queue upgrades** using Builders
3. **Train troops** and attack other villages for loot
4. **Defend** — your village gets attacked while you're offline
5. **Repeat** at higher levels

The game is primarily about resource management and time management. Understanding what to upgrade next and why is the entire skill of progressing efficiently.

## Resources Explained

### Gold
Used for: Building and upgrading defenses, traps, walls

Gold is stored in Gold Storages. Collectors gather it passively. Raiding other players is the fastest way to fill your Gold.

### Elixir
Used for: Building and upgrading troops, spells, some buildings (Army Camps, Barracks, Laboratory)

Elixir follows the same rules as Gold — passive collection + raiding.

### Dark Elixir
Used for: Hero upgrades, Dark Elixir troops (Hog Rider, Witch, etc.), some spells

Dark Elixir is the most precious resource in the game. Protect your Dark Elixir Storage — it's what attackers hunt most.

### Gems
Used for: Speeding up timers, buying resources, extra builders

**Don't spend Gems on anything except Builders.** The Builder Hut (extra builder) costs 500 Gems and is the highest-value purchase in the game. Save every Gem you earn from clearing obstacles and achievements until you have 5 builders.

## Builders Are Everything

Builders are the most important resource in COC. You start with 2. Each additional builder costs Gems:
- Builder 3: 500 Gems
- Builder 4: 1,000 Gems
- Builder 5: 2,000 Gems

**Goal:** Get 5 Builders before doing anything else with your Gems. This is the single best investment in the game. With 5 builders, you progress at 5× the rate of someone with 1 builder.

Until you have 5 builders, always have every builder active. An idle builder is wasted time.

## Town Hall — Don't Rush It

The Town Hall unlocks new buildings, troops, and content. New players want to upgrade it as fast as possible — this is the #1 beginner mistake.

**Why rushing is bad:**
- You unlock new buildings you can't afford to upgrade
- You face stronger opponents in multiplayer matchmaking
- Under-upgraded defenses make you easy 3-star loot for higher-level attackers
- Your heroes stay low-level (they're expensive and slow to upgrade)

**The rule:** Before upgrading your Town Hall, max out (or near-max) everything at your current level. This means:
- All defenses upgraded to current max
- Heroes pushed toward TH level caps
- All troops/spells you regularly use upgraded
- Walls at or near max level

Taking 2-3 months at each Town Hall level is normal and correct.

## Your First Hero: Barbarian King (TH7)

The Barbarian King unlocks at Town Hall 7. He costs Dark Elixir to build and upgrade.

**What he does:** He's a massive melee fighter who guards your base when defending and leads your army when attacking. His ability summons extra Barbarians and briefly makes him invincible.

**Key rules for heroes:**
- Keep heroes upgrading at all times — every day of idle hero is wasted Dark Elixir potential
- Heroes are down (sleeping) while upgrading — don't upgrade before a Clan War attack
- The Archer Queen (TH9) is even more powerful than the King — upgrade her first when she's available

## Attacking Basics

### Finding a Match
Tap "Attack" → "Multiplayer" to find a base to raid. You see the loot available before you commit to attacking. Scout first — look for:
- Full Gold/Elixir storages
- Low-level walls or missing defenses
- A Town Hall outside the walls (free 1-star + bonus loot)

### Deployment
Tap troops from your army panel to deploy them. You cannot cancel deployment once a troop is placed.

**Beginner attack tips:**
- Spread troops to avoid splash damage (Mortars and Wizard Towers hit groups)
- Use Wall Breakers to open wall compartments before main troops get stuck
- Deploy Giants first — they soak damage while Archers/Wizards deal damage from behind

### Stars
- 1 star: Destroy the Town Hall
- 2 stars: Destroy 50% of the base
- 3 stars: Destroy 100% of the base

In multiplayer, 1-star attacks still earn loot. In Clan War, stars determine war results.

## Defending Your Village

You can't actively defend — attacks happen while you're offline. Your job is to build a base that passively resists attacks.

**Basic defense principles:**
1. **Protect your storages.** Keep Gold, Elixir, and Dark Elixir Storages inside walls near the center.
2. **Don't leave the Town Hall outside.** An unprotected Town Hall is free loot and a free star for attackers.
3. **Cover the base with defenses.** Every part of your base should be within range of at least one defense.
4. **Use the Clan Castle.** Request troops from your Clan — they defend your base automatically when triggered.

---

## Joining a Clan

Joining a Clan is one of the most important things you can do as a new player.

**Benefits:**
- Clan Castle troops for both attack (request troops) and defense (they auto-defend)
- Clan Wars — earn bonus loot and practice against same-level opponents
- Clan Games — earn magic items (Book of Heroes, Builder Potions) as a group
- Advice and help from experienced clanmates

**How to find a clan:** Use the in-game search. Look for clans that:
- Match your Town Hall level
- Have a "Required Trophies" threshold you can meet
- Are active in Clan Games (check participation)

## Beginner Mistakes to Avoid

**Spending Gems on anything except Builders.** Speed-ups, resource refills, and magic items from Gems are a trap. 5 Builders is the only Gem purchase that pays for itself.

**Rushing Town Hall.** See above — the most common and most damaging mistake.

**Ignoring hero upgrades.** Even 10 levels on a hero dramatically changes your attack capability. Dark Elixir goes to heroes first.

**Leaving builders idle.** Every idle builder is time and progress lost forever. If you can't afford a building upgrade right now, farm more loot — but always have a target queued.

**Attacking without a plan.** Random deployment loses attacks. Pick a strategy (even "Giants + Archers" works at low levels), commit to a deployment angle, and follow through.
