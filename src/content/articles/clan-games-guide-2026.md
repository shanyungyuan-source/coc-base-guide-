## What Is Clan Games?

Clan Games is a monthly event that runs for 6 days, where all clan members complete in-game challenges to earn points for both the clan and themselves. As point thresholds are crossed, reward tiers unlock — and the top tiers contain some of the most valuable items in the game.

The key mechanic: Clan Games is **both a clan effort and an individual effort**. The clan unlocks tiers together; individual players choose which rewards from each unlocked tier to claim.

---

## How Clan Games Works

### Timeline
Clan Games runs for 6 days, typically starting mid-month (exact dates announced in-game). You'll see a notification when they begin.

### Challenges
Each player sees a list of 6 challenges at a time. Completing a challenge earns points toward the tier total. Once you complete a challenge, it disappears and a new one takes its place.

**Challenge types:**
- Win multiplayer battles
- Win Clan War attacks
- Donate troops
- Destroy X buildings in attacks
- Use specific spells/troops in battles
- Collect resources
- Complete Builder Base battles

Each challenge has a point value. Harder challenges = more points.

### Clan Tier Thresholds
The clan needs a combined total of points to unlock each tier. At Tier 6 (max), everyone in the clan can claim max rewards. A 50-person clan with most members contributing hits Tier 6 easily in 6 days.

## Reward Tiers: What You Get

Tiers 1–5 give progressively better rewards. **Tier 6 is the target** — it contains:

- **Book of Heroes** (the most valuable magic item in the game)
- Builder Potions
- Research Potions
- Resource packs
- Wall Rings

Each tier allows you to pick **one reward from that tier**. This means:

- If you want the Book of Heroes (Tier 6), you need to claim it specifically
- You also get to pick one reward from each previous tier (1–5)
- A player who hits personal max AND the clan reaches Tier 6 claims 6 rewards total

## Individual Points Required

To unlock all 6 personal tiers, you need **4,000 personal points**. This takes roughly 30–50 challenge completions depending on point values.

**Is 4,000 points realistic?** Yes, for any active player. 6 days × 15–20 minutes of challenge attempts per day easily reaches 4,000 points.

**What if I can't play much?** Even 1,000–2,000 personal points unlocks Tier 3–4 rewards. Partial participation is still valuable — skip the hardest challenges and focus on fast ones.

## Fastest Challenges to Complete

Not all challenges are created equal. Some give many points for little effort:

**Easy/fast (pick these first):**
- "Win X multiplayer battles" — do this while farming normally
- "Donate X troops" — if your clan is active, this fills quickly
- "Destroy X resource buildings" — happens naturally during raids
- "Collect X resources from collectors" — free from passive collection

**Medium difficulty:**
- "Use X of [specific troop] in battles" — bring that troop in 3–5 raids
- "Win Builder Base battles" — 3–4 BH attacks per session

**Skip or do last:**
- "3-star X bases" — requires full 3-star attacks, which takes effort
- "Win X Clan War attacks" — depends on war schedule
- Challenges requiring specific equipment or heroes you haven't upgraded

**Strategy:** Complete the 3 easiest challenges, let new ones refresh, repeat. This is more efficient than forcing through a hard challenge when 3 easy ones could appear next.

## What to Pick at Each Tier

If you reach all 6 tiers:

| Tier | Best Pick |
|---|---|
| Tier 6 | **Book of Heroes** — always |
| Tier 5 | Builder Potion (if all builders active) or Research Potion |
| Tier 4 | Builder Potion or Book of Building |
| Tier 3 | Builder Potion or Resource Pack |
| Tier 2 | Wall Rings or Resource Pack |
| Tier 1 | Whatever's available |

**If the clan only reaches Tier 4–5:** Pick Book of Fighting (troop upgrades) or Builder Potions over resource packs. Time-saving items are always worth more than raw resources.

## Book of Heroes: The Most Important Reward

The Book of Heroes from Clan Games Tier 6 instantly completes any hero upgrade. At high TH levels, hero upgrades cost 7–14 days per level. A Book of Heroes from Clan Games is worth 2 weeks of passive hero upgrading — every month.

Over 6 months: 6 Book of Heroes = 12 weeks of hero upgrades saved. This is the compounding reason to never miss Clan Games.

**Always save the Book of Heroes for your highest-priority hero.** That's almost always the Archer Queen at TH9–13, or the Legendary equipment upgrade at TH14+.

## Clan Games Etiquette

**In active clans:**
- Complete your points within the first 3–4 days so the clan reaches Tier 6 before the event ends
- Don't complete a challenge and immediately leave it — this resets others' progress on shared challenges
- Communicate if you'll be unable to participate — leaders need to know if they should recruit temporary members for the event

**For clan leaders:**
- Set a minimum personal point requirement (1,000–2,000 is reasonable) for members to qualify for Tier 6 rewards
- Kick inactive members the week before Clan Games if they haven't participated recently

## Common Clan Games Mistakes

**Waiting until the last day.** Clan Games run for 6 days. Starting on day 5 means fewer challenges completed, fewer points, and potential for the clan to miss Tier 6. Start day one.

**Picking resource packs at Tier 6.** A Gold or Elixir pack from Tier 6 is worth far less than a Book of Heroes. Always take the Book unless your heroes are all maxed.

**Skipping months because "you don't have time."** Even 30 minutes of challenge attempts across 2 sessions gets most players to Tier 4 rewards. The Book of Heroes is too valuable to skip entirely.

**Not upgrading the hero immediately after getting the Book.** If your heroes are all mid-upgrade when Clan Games end, the Book sits unused. Plan your hero upgrades around the Clan Games calendar — finish one level just before Clan Games starts so the Book can be used immediately.
