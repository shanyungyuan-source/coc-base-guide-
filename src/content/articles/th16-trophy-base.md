## Overview

At TH16, defensive power is significantly stronger, but **Super Witches** and **Dragons** remain the dominant attack compositions. The goal of a trophy base is simple: **maximize 2-star defense rates and prevent 3-stars at all costs**.

## Core Design Principles

### 1. Hero Protection

- Spread your **Barbarian King**, **Archer Queen**, **Royal Champion**, and **Battle Machine** across all four corners
- Never cluster heroes — concentrated heroes can be picked off by precision attacks
- Equip at least one Legendary item on each hero

### 2. Air Defense Placement

TH16 has **4 Air Defenses**. Recommended placement:

| Zone | Guideline |
|------|-----------|
| Core | At least 2, protecting the TH |
| Outer ring | 2 spread out to intercept split-path air attacks |

### 3. Compartment Design

Split your base into **6–8 independent compartments**:

- Place only 1–2 key defenses per compartment
- Use staggered wall layouts to slow ground troops
- Position Inferno Towers in adjacent compartments so they support each other

## Recommended Layout #1: Ring Core

**Best against**: Quick trophy farming, Super Witch spam

**Key features**:
- TH buried in the center with at least 3 wall layers on all sides
- 4 Air Defenses evenly spaced on the inner ring
- Scattershots covering all entry angles

## Recommended Layout #2: Honeycomb

**Best against**: Hybrid ground-air attacks

**Key features**:
- Base divided into 7 isolated sections
- Each section holds one defensive combo (ranged + splash)
- Eagle Artillery positioned center-rear for maximum coverage

## Common Defense Mistakes

1. **Too many wall openings** — Ground troops path freely; keep open gaps under 4
2. **Air Defenses too close to the edge** — Balloon funnels clear them before reaching the core
3. **Predictable trap placement** — Giant Bombs and Spring Traps need to cover multiple angles
4. **Heroes all on one side** — Attackers exploit the undefended half freely

## Summary

No TH16 trophy base stays unbeatable forever. Rotate your layout every 2 weeks to stay ahead of targeted attacks. Understanding *why* a layout works beats blindly copying one — use these principles to build your own and you'll consistently outperform static copies.
