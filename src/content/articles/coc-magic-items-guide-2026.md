## What Are Magic Items?

Magic items are special consumables in Clash of Clans that accelerate construction, research, and training — instantly or through time compression. They're earned through Clan Games, season passes, events, and League rewards.

Used correctly, magic items can save weeks of upgrade time. Used wastefully, they're wasted on upgrades that wouldn't have blocked your progress anyway.

This guide covers every major magic item, when to use each one, and the mistakes that waste them.

---

## Builder-Related Items

### Builder Potion
**Effect:** All 5 Builders work at 10× speed for 1 hour (equivalent to ~10 hours of building time)

**When to use:**
- When all 5 builders are actively upgrading (never use it with idle builders)
- On long, expensive upgrades — a Builder Potion on a 10-day wall upgrade saves more time than on a 6-hour barrack upgrade
- Stack multiple potions during a Clan Games rush to max progress fast

**When NOT to use:**
- If any builder is idle — you lose the full value of that builder's 10× time
- Right before sleep — the 1-hour window goes wasted

### Book of Building
**Effect:** Instantly completes any one building or upgrade (no time limit on the upgrade)

**Highest value targets:**
1. Your longest active building upgrade (Eagle Artillery, Scattershot, Inferno Tower)
2. A new TH unlock you just built to skip the initial upgrade time
3. Wall upgrades (each wall is a "building") — a Book of Building on a level 14 wall costs nothing and saves a day

**Never use on:** Anything under 1 day remaining — potions are better for short upgrades.

### Rune of Builder Gold / Builder Elixir
**Effect:** Completely fills your Builder Base Gold or Builder Elixir storage

**When to use:** When you have active Builder Base upgrades queued and empty storages. Most effective when used before a long offline period so resources don't overflow.

## Research Items

### Research Potion
**Effect:** Laboratory works at 24× speed for 1 hour (~24 hours of research time)

**When to use:**
- On troops or spells you actively use that have long research times
- Stack with Book of Fighting if you're close to finishing a key troop

**Best targets:** Root Rider, Super Witch (underlying Witch), Electro Dragon, Yeti — long research times with high meta impact.

### Book of Fighting
**Effect:** Instantly completes any troop or spell upgrade

**Highest value targets:**
1. Your highest-priority attack troop mid-upgrade (Root Rider, Electro Dragon)
2. Any troop upgrade over 12 days remaining
3. Hero Equipment upgrades at the Blacksmith

**Avoid using on:** Level 1→2 upgrades for troops you rarely use. Save it for the top of your upgrade queue.

### Book of Spells
**Effect:** Instantly completes any spell upgrade

**Same principle as Book of Fighting** — use it on spells that are long and actively used. Earthquake Spell and Bat Spell are strong targets.

## Hero Items

### Book of Heroes
**Effect:** Instantly completes any hero upgrade

**This is the most valuable item in the game.** Hero upgrades cost Dark Elixir and take 7-14 days at high levels. A Book of Heroes on a Barbarian King level 70 upgrade is worth more than 12 days of build time.

**Priority order for Book of Heroes:**
1. Archer Queen — every level matters most
2. Grand Warden — key level thresholds (L20, L40) unlock major Eternal Tome radius improvements
3. Barbarian King — push alongside Queen
4. Royal Champion — use after the above three

**Timing tip:** Use Book of Heroes right before a Clan War League season starts so your heroes are available at full HP for wars.

### Hero Potion
**Effect:** All heroes temporarily boosted to level 15 equivalent for 1 hour

**When to use:** During Clan War League when you're not confident in your low-level heroes. A Hero Potion gives you a taste of max-level hero power temporarily.

**Important:** Hero Potions don't work if the hero is upgrading. Use a Book of Heroes first if needed.

## Training Items

### Training Potion
**Effect:** All troops and spells instantly finish training for 1 hour

**Best use:** During Clan War League (CWL) when you need to attack 8+ times in a season. Stack Training Potions to keep your army always ready.

**Value per situation:**
- CWL season: Extremely high — you attack frequently and army cook time is the bottleneck
- Legend League: High — 8 daily attacks need constant army readiness
- Farming: Low — you usually have time to wait between raids

### Power Potion
**Effect:** All troops and heroes temporarily boosted to your TH's max level for 1 hour

**When to use:** Friendly challenges to test higher-level strategies, or boosting before a critical CWL attack where your troop levels are just below the threshold for a key strategy.

## Clan Games Reward Priority

If you can only complete a limited number of Clan Games tiers, prioritize rewards in this order:

1. **Book of Heroes** — Highest single-item value in the game
2. **Book of Building / Book of Fighting** — Saves the most time on long upgrades
3. **Builder Potion × 5** — Collectively equivalent to 2+ days of construction
4. **Research Potion** — Strong if you're actively leveling attack troops

## Common Magic Item Mistakes

**Using a Builder Potion with an idle builder.** Each idle builder cuts the potion's value. A 4-builder potion use is 80% efficient; a 3-builder use is 60%. Always fill all builders before activating.

**Saving items forever "for the right moment."** Items in storage do nothing. A good use now beats a perfect use never — especially Builder and Research Potions with expiry risk.

**Using Book of Heroes on low-level hero upgrades.** A Book of Heroes on level 5→6 King saves 20 minutes. Save it for the level 65→70 stretch where each level takes 10+ days.

**Using Training Potion between wars.** Training Potion is wasted if your army only needs to be ready once every few days. Use it during CWL where the value multiplies across 8+ attacks.
