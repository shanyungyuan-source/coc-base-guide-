## TH15 War Base Overview

TH15 is one of the most populated Town Hall levels in Clash of Clans. War base design differs fundamentally from trophy bases:

- **War base goal**: Force a **0-star or 1-star** — sacrifice resources, never give up 3 stars
- **Trophy base goal**: Accept 2 stars, but stop the 3-star at all costs

## Current Meta Attack Compositions

### Super Witch + Dragon (Monkey Witch)

**Weakness**: Relies on a wide funnel — multiple tight compartments break the funnel

**Counter layout**:
- Dense inner compartments to trap the funnel
- Inferno Towers (single-target) on Dragon corridors
- Skeleton Traps set to **Ground mode** to distract Witches mid-push

### Hydra (Super Dragon + Super Minion)

**Weakness**: Pure air composition, ground defenses are irrelevant

**Counter layout**:
- All Air Defenses pushed into the core
- Air Bombs layered along common flight paths
- Scattershot and Monolith prioritized to protect the TH

### Yeti + Goblin Ground Army

**Weakness**: Slow movement, depends on tanky units to open the base

**Counter layout**:
- Giant Bombs and Land Mines clustered on the outer ring
- Skeleton Traps (ground mode) placed beside heroes
- Scattershot aimed at high-HP ground units

## Advanced Technique: Anti-Funnel Design

A **funnel** is the corridor attackers build before pushing the core. **Anti-funnel layouts** deny this by:

1. **Circular base shape** — No obvious corners, forces attackers to deal with all four sides simultaneously
2. **Scattered heroes** — Heroes at four corners force troop splitting
3. **Fake core** — High-value-looking buildings on the outer edge lure attackers into wasting troops off-path

## How to Test Your War Base

After each war:
1. Watch the attack replays — identify exactly which compartment failed first
2. Adjust layout to address that specific weakness
3. Cross-reference with top-rated TH15 war bases on [CocBase](https://www.cocbase.com/) to find common patterns

## Conclusion

There's no universal war base. The key is **continuous iteration**. Spend 5 minutes after every war reviewing attack replays, pinpoint the weak spot, and tweak. Layouts that evolve with the meta consistently outperform static ones.
