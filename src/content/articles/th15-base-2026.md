The Best TH15 Base Layouts for 2026: War, Farming & Trophy Strategies
Town Hall 15 introduces the Monolith — the most powerful single defense in the game — along with the Hero Equipment system that completely transforms how heroes perform in both attack and defense. The th15 base meta in 2026 is defined by Root Rider ground attacks, Super Witch combos with Legendary equipment, and precision air strategies targeting the Monolith. This guide covers every threat your TH15 base will face.

What Makes a Strong TH15 Base?
Core Design Principles
The Monolith is TH15's game-changing defense. It targets the highest-hitpoint troop on the field and deals damage based on a percentage of that troop's maximum hitpoints — meaning it scales perfectly against tanks like Golems, Super Giants, and high-level Heroes. Place it in the absolute core of your base where it can fire continuously throughout the entire attack.
A strong th15 base in 2026 accounts for Hero Equipment. Heroes equipped with Legendary items like the Giant Gauntlet or Rocket Spear deal and absorb dramatically more damage than unequipped heroes. Your Inferno Towers should always be on single-target mode in war — they are the most effective counter to high-hitpoint hero builds regardless of equipment level.
Three Scattershots at TH15 form the backbone of area denial. Treat them as a triangle: one covering the left approach, one covering the right, and one covering the rear. Their combined splash coverage should reach every tile of the map from at least one Scattershot at all times.
Defensive Building Placement
Five Air Defenses at TH15 enable true air denial when properly spread. Place them in a pentagon formation — four at the corners and one central — so no air attack can path through more than two simultaneously. The central Air Defense covers any gaps between the corner placements.
The Eagle Artillery at TH15 activates the moment an attacker deploys their hundredth troop. Central placement means it fires on every attack from the first troop until it's destroyed — attackers who try to snipe it early face your full base layout with a depleted army.

TH15 War Base Strategy: Stopping 3-Stars
Anti-Root Rider Setup
Root Riders are TH15's most dangerous ground troop. They ignore walls entirely, walking through them as if they don't exist — making traditional compartment design less effective against this specific threat. The counter is overlapping defense coverage rather than wall reliance.
Place your Scattershots with overlapping coverage zones specifically around the most valuable core defenses. Root Riders that walk through walls immediately face Scattershot fire from multiple directions. A Giant Bomb pair buried inside each major wall section — not at the edges — triggers when Root Riders pass through, dealing damage that ignores their wall-bypass ability.
The Monolith positioned directly adjacent to the Town Hall creates a final kill zone that Root Riders reaching the core must overcome. At TH15 levels, the Monolith's percentage-based damage eliminates even the highest-hitpoint Root Riders within seconds of engagement.
Anti-Super Witch with Legendary Equipment
Super Witch attacks powered by Legendary Hero Equipment have dramatically increased damage output in 2026. The standard Bomb Tower counter remains effective, but needs reinforcement — place two Bomb Towers along the primary Super Witch push path rather than one.
Use an asymmetric inner core design. Super Witch attacks rely on predictable compartment-clearing patterns — an asymmetric core forces the attack into unfamiliar paths where your trap placement is optimized and the Witches can't maintain formation.

TH15 Farming Base: Protecting Your Resources
Dark Elixir and Equipment Shards
TH15 introduces Equipment Shards alongside traditional Dark Elixir requirements. Both resources fund hero progression — protecting the Dark Elixir Storage means protecting your entire upgrade economy. Bury it behind the Monolith, both central Inferno Towers, and the Eagle Artillery.
A double Hidden Tesla layer adjacent to the Dark Elixir Storage creates the most protected resource position possible at TH15. Attackers who reach this cluster face percentage-based Monolith damage, single-target Inferno fire, Eagle Artillery targeting, and surprise Tesla activation simultaneously.
Multi-Resource Strategy
Seven Gold Storages and seven Elixir Storages at TH15 require a four-tier distribution. Place two of each in the outer ring as bait, two in the second ring, two in the third ring, and one of each in the core. This structure means even a successful three-star attack struggles to loot more than sixty percent of total resources.

5 Recommended TH15 Base Layout Styles
1. The Monolith Fortress
Monolith placed in the exact center, flanked by both Inferno Towers and the Eagle Artillery. Three Scattershots form a protective triangle around this central cluster. Any attack that reaches the Monolith faces the combined fire of the most powerful defenses at TH15 simultaneously.
2. The Anti-Root Rider Base
Overlapping Scattershot coverage with no gaps anywhere on the map. Giant Bomb pairs buried inside every major wall section rather than at edges. Root Riders that pass through walls immediately enter multi-directional Scattershot fire zones. Consistently stops the dominant TH15 ground strategy.
3. The Eagle Artillery Core
Eagle Artillery positioned centrally to fire from the first hundred troops deployed to the last. Five Scattershots and both Inferno Towers provide layered protection. Attackers face Eagle Artillery pressure throughout the entire attack duration, dramatically increasing troop consumption before the army reaches the core.
4. The Air Supremacy Defense
Five Air Defenses in maximum pentagon spread, all Scattershots on air-targeting mode, Seeking Air Mines at every Air Defense perimeter. Destroys every air strategy at TH15. Forces opponents into ground attacks against optimal trap placement and three Scattershots with fresh positioning.
5. The Equipment Counter Base
Specifically designed to counter Legendary Equipment hero builds. Single-target Inferno Towers placed on opposite sides of the base so any Hero Walk immediately faces Inferno fire. Tornado Traps at every corner entry point. No safe walk path exists from any direction.

Common TH15 Base Mistakes to Avoid
Placing the Monolith in the outer ring is the worst TH15 base error possible. The Monolith costs immense resources to build and upgrade — if attackers can destroy it in the first thirty seconds without committing their full army, your base loses its most powerful defense before the real attack even begins.
Building a wall-dependent base at TH15 without accounting for Root Riders leaves you vulnerable to the most common attack strategy at this level. Every base design at TH15 needs Scattershot overlap coverage that functions independently of walls, not just with them.
Ignoring the Hero Equipment meta in base design means your layout was built for a game that no longer exists. TH15 heroes with Legendary Equipment absorb and deal damage that TH14-era base designs simply cannot handle. Audit your base specifically against current equipment loadouts.
