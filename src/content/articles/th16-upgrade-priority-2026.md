## TH16 Upgrade Overview

TH16 is the current max Town Hall and the endpoint of the main village progression. There's no Town Hall 17 waiting ahead — which changes the upgrade mindset significantly. Instead of racing toward the next unlock, TH16 is about deepening existing systems: maxing hero equipment, fully upgrading the Giga Inferno weapon, and ensuring every defense is at its ceiling.

The biggest mistake TH16 players make is continuing to treat it like a mid-game TH — rushing through buildings while neglecting equipment. At TH16, the Blacksmith is as important as any Builder.

---

## First 24 Hours at TH16

1. **Upgrade Town Hall weapon to Giga Inferno** — The TH16 weapon upgrade is one of the longest single timers in the game. Start it immediately.
2. **Build all new defensive slots** — TH16 adds additional building capacity. Fill every slot before upgrading.
3. **Upgrade Spell Tower** — TH16 Spell Tower reaches its most powerful level. It should be a day-one build target.
4. **Assess equipment** — Open the Blacksmith and check your equipment levels against the TH16 war-ready benchmark (Giant Gauntlet 18+, Frozen Arrow 18+).

## Priority 1: Hero Equipment (Endgame)

At TH16 there are no more hero level unlocks beyond the current cap. The final frontier of hero power is **equipment levels**.

**War-ready TH16 equipment benchmarks:**

| Hero | Equipment | Target Level |
|---|---|---|
| Barbarian King | Giant Gauntlet | 18–20 |
| Barbarian King | Spiky Ball | 15+ |
| Archer Queen | Frozen Arrow | 18–20 |
| Grand Warden | Healing Tome | 15+ |
| Grand Warden | Life Gem | 15+ |
| Royal Champion | Hog Rider Puppet | 15+ |

Upgrading Legendary equipment from level 15 to 18+ at TH16 is a meaningful DPS and survivability jump — not cosmetic. These upgrades are the primary progression content at TH16.

**Ore sources at TH16:**
- Clan Games max tier (every month, never miss it)
- Raid Weekend Capital Gold → Starry Ore exchange
- Seasonal event challenges (check the event tab weekly)
- Achievement completions that award ore

## Priority 2: Giga Inferno (Town Hall Weapon)

The TH16 Giga Inferno at max level fires a multi-target beam that deals catastrophic damage to any army cluster approaching the Town Hall. At lower levels it's a single-target beam — at max level, multiple beams.

**Why rush the Giga Inferno:**
- It's a permanent passive defense on the Town Hall itself
- Level 5 Giga Inferno significantly reduces 3-star rates on well-designed bases
- Unlike other defenses, it doesn't require a Builder — it's the Town Hall upgrade itself

Start it day one and let it run in the background while Builders handle external defenses.

---

## Priority 3: Scattershots and Eagle Artillery

TH16's Scattershots and Eagle Artillery are the defenses attackers most want to eliminate first — because they're the most dangerous to ground and mixed armies respectively.

**Max these before other defenses:**
1. Both Scattershots to max — TH16 Scattershots in anti-air mode counter Super Dragon Zap; in ground mode they're the primary Root Rider counter
2. Eagle Artillery to max — Every level improves burst damage and radius
3. Inferno Towers — Max both; multi-target Infernos are essential vs. Super Witch clusters

---

## Priority 4: Spell Tower

The TH16 Spell Tower at max level can either freeze enemies mid-attack or speed up your own CC troops. In a war defense context, **Freeze mode** is almost always correct — a well-timed freeze stops a Root Rider + Super Witch push cold and often means the difference between a 2-star and 3-star defense.

Get the Spell Tower to level 3+ early in TH16. The level threshold at level 3 widens the freeze radius meaningfully.

**Placement tip:** Centralize the Spell Tower so its freeze radius covers both the Town Hall compartment and the most likely push lane. A Spell Tower firing from outside the core base is a much less effective.

---

## Priority 5: Air Defenses and X-Bows

TH16 has the most Air Defenses and X-Bows in the game. Maxing all of them is a long project — approach it systematically:

- Do **one side** of your base at a time (all Air Defenses on the left side, then right)
- This ensures your base never has a completely undefended attack angle while upgrades are running
- X-Bows to max in parallel — at TH16, X-Bows reach level 12, with meaningful HP and damage upgrades

---

## Priority 6: Heroes

TH16 heroes max out at high levels (King 90, Queen 90, Warden 70, RC 50, Druid 40). If you arrived at TH16 under-leveled, continue the grind — but expect it to be a 3-6 month process.

**Dark Elixir allocation:** Same hierarchy as lower TH levels.
1. Archer Queen first
2. Grand Warden (Elixir — never idle)
3. Barbarian King
4. Royal Champion
5. Druid (Elixir — never idle)

The difference between a level 80 Queen and level 90 Queen at TH16 is meaningful in war — each level adds stats plus the equipment scaling multiplier.

## Common TH16 Upgrade Mistakes

**Neglecting equipment upgrades.** At TH16, many players fall back on old habits — builders on buildings, heroes upgrading, equipment ignored. Equipment at TH16 is the highest-value upgrade per resource spent. The Blacksmith should always have something upgrading.

**Putting the Spell Tower in a corner.** A corner Spell Tower only affects attacks coming from that angle. Centralize it so its freeze radius covers the widest possible section of the base interior.

**Spreading builders across too many low-priority defenses.** With 5 builders and many upgrades available, it's tempting to spread work across Cannons, Archer Towers, Mortars, and Wizard Towers simultaneously. This delays your highest-impact defenses. Finish Scattershots, Eagle, and Infernos first — then spread to the rest.
