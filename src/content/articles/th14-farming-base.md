## Why Farming Bases Matter

Most players obsess over trophy pushing and war performance while ignoring **resource efficiency**. At TH14, upgrading heroes and buildings requires massive amounts of:

- 🌑 **Dark Elixir** — Essential for every hero level
- 💛 **Gold** — Walls and building upgrades
- 💧 **Elixir** — Troop and spell upgrades

A good farming base minimizes resource losses when attacked — especially Dark Elixir.

## Dark Elixir: Top Priority

Each Dark Elixir storage holds only 5,000 DE, and a high percentage gets stolen per raid. Maxing out the Barbarian King, Archer Queen, and Royal Champion at TH14 costs hundreds of thousands of Dark Elixir — making DE storage protection your single most important design goal.

> **Rule**: Dark Elixir Storages must sit in the core, behind at least two full wall layers.

## Farming Base Design Principles

### Distributed Storage

- Spread Gold Storages and Elixir Storages across **separate compartments**
- Even if one zone gets wiped, the others remain intact
- TH14 has 6 Gold Storages + 6 Elixir Storages — group them in 3 clusters at three points of a triangle

### Town Hall Positioning

In a farming base, the TH can go **on the outside**:

- An outside TH gives up only 1 star and a small resource bonus
- Many attackers grab the 1 star and leave (conserving their troops)
- The core is then dedicated entirely to protecting DE storages

```
Outer ring:  TH + light defenses (bait)
Middle ring: Gold + Elixir Storages
Core:        Dark Elixir Storage × 2 + Hero Altars
```

### Key Defensive Buildings for Resource Zones

| Building | Role |
|----------|------|
| Giant Bomb × 4 | Wipes Goblin squads rushing storages |
| Spring Trap | Launches Goblins before they reach DE |
| Seeking Air Mine | Counters Elite Goblin air drops |
| X-Bow (ground mode) | High-efficiency anti-infantry coverage |

## Typical Farming Base Structure

```
      [DE Drill]   [DE Drill]
 [Gold]                  [Gold]
      [DE Storage] TH14 [DE Storage]
 [Elix]                  [Elix]
      [Gold]   [Gold]
```

Place the TH and Builder Huts on the outer ring as bait, so attackers burn through their army clearing the perimeter — leaving nothing left to crack the core.

## Countering Goblin Knife

The most common farming raid at TH14 is **Goblin Knife** — using Lightning Spells or Balloons to clear outer defenses, then flooding the base with Goblins.

**How to counter it**:
1. Place 2–3 Giant Bombs around every storage cluster
2. Set X-Bows to ground mode pointed at storage-dense zones
3. Archer Queen at level 60+ handles Goblin squads reliably

## Upgrade Priority at TH14

If you just arrived at TH14, this order maximizes efficiency:

1. **Archer Queen** (push to level 75 first — best DE-per-impact value)
2. **Dark Elixir Drills** × 2 (boost daily passive DE income)
3. **X-Bows** × 4 (strongest ground defense in storage zones)
4. **Walls** (level 13 gold walls — massive HP jump)

## Summary

A good farming base pays for itself quickly. Saving an extra 2,000–3,000 DE per day compounds into weeks of saved upgrade time over a month. Ten minutes rearranging your base is always worth it.
