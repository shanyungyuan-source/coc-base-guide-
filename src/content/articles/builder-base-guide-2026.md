## What Is the Builder Base?

The Builder Base is a separate village in Clash of Clans with its own troops, buildings, heroes, and currency (Builder Gold and Builder Elixir). It runs independently from your main village — you can work on both simultaneously.

The key difference: Builder Base uses a **versus battle** system. You attack an opponent's base and they automatically attack yours. Whoever scores more stars (or more destruction in a tie) wins the match and earns Builder Gold.

---

## Why the Builder Base Matters

**The O.T.T.O Hut.** This is the main reason to invest in the Builder Base. Fully upgrading specific BH buildings unlocks the O.T.T.O Hut, which provides a 6th builder for your **main village**. A 6th builder is a permanent, massive progression boost — it's worth every resource you put into the Builder Base to unlock it.

**O.T.T.O requirements (as of 2026):**
- Gear Up: 3 main village defenses using BH Gold (requires BH5+)
- Multi Mortar to level 8
- Cannon Cart to level 18
- Mega Tesla to level 9
- Battle Machine to level 30

## Builder Base Basics

### Resources
- **Builder Gold:** Used for buildings and defenses in BH
- **Builder Elixir:** Used for troops in BH

Both are earned through Versus Battles and collected passively from Builder Mines and Builder Elixir Collectors.

### The Battle Machine
The Battle Machine is the Builder Base hero. He's an Elixir-powered hero with a powerful ability (Electric Hammer) that deals massive damage and temporarily boosts him. Upgrade him constantly — he has a higher impact at BH than heroes do at main village at early levels.

### Clock Tower
The Clock Tower temporarily speeds up all BH resource collection and building at 10× speed. Use it strategically — activate when all your builders are busy with long upgrades for maximum benefit.

## BH Upgrade Priority

### BH5-7: Focus on Battle Machine
The Battle Machine is the most impactful upgrade in the early BH. Get him to level 15+ before upgrading most other buildings.

**Priority:**
1. Battle Machine upgrades
2. Multi Mortar (toward O.T.T.O requirement)
3. Cannon Cart (toward O.T.T.O requirement)

### BH8-9: Unlock and Upgrade New Troops
BH8 unlocks new attack options. The key new troop is the **Super P.E.K.K.A** (BH version).

**Priority:**
1. Battle Machine to level 25+
2. Mega Tesla upgrades (toward O.T.T.O requirement)
3. New defensive buildings to current max

### BH10: Complete O.T.T.O
BH10 is the current max. The goal here is completing all O.T.T.O requirements to get the 6th Builder.

**Priority:**
1. Cannon Cart to level 18 (often the longest grind)
2. Battle Machine to level 30
3. Mega Tesla to level 9
4. All defenses to max

## Best BH Attack Strategies

### Mass Giant (BH5-7)
Deploy Giants across the base front with Archers behind for ranged DPS. Giants tank, Archers kill. Simple, consistent, effective at lower BH levels.

### Battle Machine Walk (BH7-8)
Use the Battle Machine to walk one side of the base, deleting buildings while his ability keeps him alive. Deploy supporting troops on the other side to push simultaneously.

### Super P.E.K.K.A + Cannon Cart (BH8-9)
Cannon Carts target defenses and have high DPS. Super P.E.K.K.A has massive HP and damage. Together they push through BH8-9 bases reliably.

**Deploy:** Send Cannon Carts at defenses on one side, Super P.E.K.K.A in the center, Battle Machine behind the Carts to clean what they miss.

### Night Witch Spam (BH9-10)
Night Witches spawn Bats continuously and deal significant area damage. At high levels, 8 Night Witches flooding bats across a base clear it efficiently with minimal spell support.

## Clock Tower Strategy

The Clock Tower activates for 14 minutes (upgradeable), speeding up all BH activity to 10×. To maximize value:

1. Only activate when **all BH builders are busy** on long upgrades
2. If the Clock Tower is ready and builders are idle, you're losing value — queue upgrades first
3. Activate before logging off for a long stretch — 14 minutes of 10× = 140 minutes of effective progress per activation

**Clock Tower Potion** (from Clan Games): Instantly fills the Clock Tower's timer. Use it when all builders are on long BH upgrades for maximum time compression.

## Common Builder Base Mistakes

**Ignoring the BH for months.** The O.T.T.O 6th Builder is one of the best permanent upgrades in the game. Every day you ignore the BH is a day closer to the 6th Builder you're not taking.

**Leaving the Battle Machine idle.** Same rule as main village heroes — always keep the Battle Machine upgrading. His upgrades are cheap and fast at low levels.

**Activating Clock Tower with idle builders.** An idle builder during Clock Tower activation is pure waste. Fill all builder slots before activating.

**Under-upgrading Cannon Cart.** The Cannon Cart requirement for O.T.T.O (level 18) is the longest grind. Start it early and upgrade it consistently — don't leave it until last.
