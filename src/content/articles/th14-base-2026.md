The Best TH14 Base Layouts for 2026: War, Farming & Trophy Strategies
Town Hall 14 brings the Pet House and Hero Pets into the game, fundamentally changing how attacks work. The th14 base meta in 2026 is dominated by Super Witch attacks, Hybrid ground strategies using Pets, and Electro Dragon combinations powered by the new equipment system. Your base needs to counter all of it. This guide covers the full TH14 picture.

What Makes a Strong TH14 Base?
Core Design Principles
TH14 introduces the Pet House, which allows heroes to equip pets like L.A.S.S.I, Mighty Yak, Electro Owl, and Unicorn. These pets dramatically increase hero survivability during attacks — which means your base needs to account for Heroes that can sustain significantly more damage before going down.
A strong th14 base in 2026 keeps all Inferno Towers on single-target mode for war. Hero + Pet combinations have enormous hitpoint pools — a Barbarian King with a Mighty Yak and a Unicorn healing him can survive for an extremely long time. Single-target Inferno Tower fire burns through this combined health pool faster than any other defense at TH14.
The Scattershot upgrade at TH14 adds a second unit to your base. Treat both Scattershots as the backbone of your air and ground splash coverage — position them on opposite sides of the base so any attack through the center faces fire from both simultaneously.
Defensive Building Placement
Four Air Defenses at TH14 need to cover the full base width in a diamond formation. Super Witch attacks send a Minion air layer alongside the ground push — your Air Defenses need clear coverage to eliminate this air support before it clears compartments for the main ground force.
The Giga Inferno on your Town Hall at TH14 deals massive damage and switches to multi-target mode at higher levels. Keep the Town Hall buried in the core — the Giga Inferno's explosion on destruction damages surrounding troops, making the Town Hall itself a dangerous final obstacle.

TH14 War Base Strategy: Stopping 3-Stars
Anti-Super Witch Setup
Super Witch attacks are the dominant strategy at TH14 in Clan War. Super Witches summon skeleton armies that clear compartments while the Witches deal damage from range. The key counter is a base design that forces Super Witches into a single narrow entry point rather than a broad front.
Place Bomb Towers at every major compartment junction. When a Bomb Tower dies in the path of a Super Witch push, its explosion hits both the skeleton armies and the Witches simultaneously, removing the protective skeleton layer and exposing the Witches to your Inferno Towers and Scattershots.
Use a Scattershot on ground-targeting mode on the most likely Super Witch entry side. TH14 Scattershot splash damage covers a wide area and eliminates skeleton clusters before they can clear walls for the main push, buying critical time for your Inferno Towers to burn through the Witches themselves.
Anti-Hybrid Pet Strategy
Hybrid attacks at TH14 combine ground troops with Hero + Pet combinations for a Queen Walk or RC Walk opener. To counter this, place single-target Inferno Towers on the edges of your base facing the most common walk entry corners.
A Hero Walk that encounters a single-target Inferno Tower loses health rapidly even with Pet support. Add a Tornado Trap adjacent to the most likely walk path — when triggered, it spins the Hero and supporting Healers into a tight cluster where your concentrated defenses eliminate them before the main army deploys.

TH14 Farming Base: Protecting Your Resources
Dark Elixir at TH14
Hero upgrades at TH14 cost enormous amounts of Dark Elixir — all four heroes need continuous upgrading. The Dark Elixir Storage goes in the absolute center of your base, deeper than the Giga Inferno Town Hall, surrounded by both Inferno Towers and a Scattershot.
Adding two Hidden Teslas adjacent to the Dark Elixir Storage creates a surprise defensive layer that triggers when attackers get within four tiles. An attacker who fights through your entire base only to face two Hidden Teslas plus both Inferno Towers at the storage often fails to loot meaningful Dark Elixir amounts.
Resource Distribution at TH14
Six Gold Storages and six Elixir Storages require careful placement. Use a three-tier system: two of each type in the outer ring as bait, two in the second ring behind real defenses, and two in the inner ring requiring a committed push. Most farming attacks loot the outer tier and leave, protecting your most concentrated resource reserves.

5 Recommended TH14 Base Layout Styles
1. The Anti-Super Witch Fortress
Tight compartmentalization with Bomb Towers at every junction. Scattershot on ground mode facing the primary Super Witch entry angle. Both Inferno Towers on single-target in the core. This layout consistently forces two-star results against the dominant TH14 attack strategy.
2. The Giga Inferno Core
Town Hall buried in the exact center, surrounded by both Scattershots and both Inferno Towers. Attackers who reach the core face the Giga Inferno explosion plus concentrated defensive fire simultaneously. Extremely difficult to three-star without perfect army composition and execution.
3. The Anti-Hero Walk Perimeter
No high-value defenses in the outer ring — all major defenses sit at least two compartments deep. Hero Walk strategies find nothing worth targeting from outside the walls and must commit the full army to reach anything meaningful. Tornado Traps at each corner deny easy entry points.
4. The Air-Denial Base
All four Air Defenses at maximum spread, supplemented by both Scattershots on air mode and Seeking Air Mines at every Air Defense perimeter. Destroys every air attack composition at TH14 and forces opponents into ground attacks against a fresh trap layout.
5. The Hybrid Resource-War Base
Equal emphasis on protecting Dark Elixir and maintaining war performance. Town Hall semi-exposed to trigger shields during farming season. Dark Elixir surrounded by single-target Infernos for maximum protection. Performs consistently across both war and farming contexts.

Common TH14 Base Mistakes to Avoid
Ignoring Pet combinations when designing your base is the most significant TH14 error. Bases built for TH13-style attacks don't account for the dramatically increased hero survivability that Pets provide. Any base where a Hero Walk can comfortably snipe outer defenses needs immediate redesign.
Placing both Scattershots on the same side of the base wastes their cross-coverage potential. A single Rocket Balloon combination can take out both if they're adjacent. Always place them on opposite corners or sides for maximum map coverage.
Not upgrading the Giga Inferno Town Hall alongside other defenses is a mistake many TH14 players make. The Giga Inferno deals significantly more damage at higher levels and its explosion becomes increasingly powerful — it should be upgraded in parallel with Scattershots and Inferno Towers, not left behind.
