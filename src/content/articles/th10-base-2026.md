The Best TH10 Base Layouts for 2026: War, Farming & Trophy Strategies
Town Hall 10 is where Clash of Clans gets serious. The th10 base meta in 2026 centers around Electro Dragon attacks, LaLoon strategies, and Hybrid Queen Walks. You now have the Inferno Towers, a second X-Bow, and the game-changing Giga Tesla on your Town Hall. How you arrange these defenses determines whether attackers walk away with two stars or three.

What Makes a Strong TH10 Base?
Core Design Principles
At TH10, the Giga Tesla on your Town Hall changes everything. Your Town Hall is now a weapon — hiding it deep in the core means attackers have to fight through your entire base to trigger and destroy it. Never leave your Town Hall exposed at TH10.
A strong th10 base in 2026 uses multi-layered compartments with specific kill zones at each layer. The first layer absorbs cheap spam attacks. The second layer handles Hog Riders and Miners. The core is a fortress designed to stop Queen Walks and P.E.K.K.A pushes.
Both Inferno Towers should be on single-target mode for war. Multi-target is tempting for farming because it hits many troops at once, but in war, single-target mode destroys Heroes and high-hitpoint troops before they do serious damage.
Defensive Building Placement
The second X-Bow at TH10 should be placed on the opposite side of the base from your first. Cross-coverage between the two X-Bows means any ground attack on any side of the base immediately faces X-Bow fire.
Air Sweepers should face outward toward the most common air attack entry points. At TH10, Electro Dragon attacks almost always come from the corners — position your Air Sweeper to push them away from your Air Defenses.

TH10 War Base Strategy: Stopping 3-Stars
Anti-Electro Dragon Setup
Electro Dragons deal chain lightning damage — they hit one target and the bolt jumps to nearby buildings. The counter is spacing. Buildings that are more than five tiles apart break the chain. Design your th10 base with deliberate gaps between clusters of buildings, especially in the outer ring.
Keep your four Air Defenses spread across the entire base in a diamond pattern. An Electro Dragon attack that can't find a clean path to two Air Defenses simultaneously will stall out and fail to three-star even with Rage Spells.
Seeking Air Mines should be placed just outside each Air Defense. When Balloons sent ahead to trigger defenses hit these mines, the entire air attack loses momentum before reaching the core.
Anti-Queen Walk Defense
A Queen Walk at TH10 typically starts from a corner and pushes toward the center, sniping defenses along the way. To stop it, place a single-target Inferno Tower within range of your base's most likely Queen Walk entry corner.
Create "trap corridors" along the edges of your base — sections that look walkable but contain Spring Traps timed to catch the Healer group supporting the Queen. Losing two Healers mid-walk usually forces attackers to abandon the strategy early.

TH10 Farming Base: Protecting Your Resources
Dark Elixir Storage Placement
At TH10, you're saving Dark Elixir for hero upgrades that cost hundreds of thousands. The Dark Elixir Storage goes in the absolute center of your base, behind your Town Hall, with both Inferno Towers between it and the nearest wall entrance.
A useful trick: place one Inferno Tower on multi-target mode specifically for farming. It shreds the large troop groups that farming attackers typically use, making your base too expensive to raid efficiently.
Gold and Elixir Storage Layout
Five Storages of each type need distributing at TH10. Use the outer two rings for Elixir Storages — they're worth less than Gold and serve as bait. Keep your Gold Storages in the second ring, close enough to defenses to require real effort to reach.

5 Recommended TH10 Base Layout Styles
1. The Anti-Electro Diamond Base
Four Air Defenses placed in a true diamond, each separated by maximum distance. Buildings in the outer ring are spaced to break Electro Dragon chain lightning. Both Inferno Towers on single-target guard the core. Consistently forces two-stars against air-heavy attackers.
2. The Inferno Core Base
Both Inferno Towers and the Town Hall form a tight triangle in the very center of the base. Three rings of compartments surround them. Ground attacks that breach the outer two rings still face the most powerful defensive combination in the game when they reach the core.
3. The Anti-LaLoon Base
Lava Hounds and Balloons require clear paths to Air Defenses. This layout places Air Defenses behind double wall layers with Air Bombs and Seeking Air Mines forming a perimeter around each one. Balloons that lose the Lava Hound shield get destroyed before reaching any Air Defense.
4. The Hybrid War-Farming Base
Town Hall sits in the center but is slightly more accessible than a pure war base. Storages are distributed evenly so no single attack can loot more than thirty percent of total resources. Performs well in both war and regular farming seasons.
5. The Funnel Denial Base
Designed specifically to prevent Queen Walk funnels. Outer compartments are shaped to push attacking troops toward Giant Bomb clusters. The base perimeter has no clean corners to start a funnel from, forcing attackers into high-risk entry points.

Common TH10 Base Mistakes to Avoid
Leaving the Giga Tesla Town Hall exposed is the biggest TH10 mistake. The Giga Tesla deals enormous damage and explodes on destruction — if attackers can reach it easily, they use it as a stepping stone rather than fighting through your whole base.
Using both Inferno Towers on multi-target in war is another common error. Multi-target looks impressive but doesn't stop Heroes or P.E.K.K.As efficiently. Single-target on both is the correct war configuration at TH10.
Not upgrading Air Defenses before other defenses is a mistake many TH10 players make. Air attacks are dominant in the current meta — an under-leveled Air Defense gets destroyed in seconds, opening the entire base to air attacks that should have been stopped.
