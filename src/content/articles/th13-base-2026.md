The Best TH13 Base Layouts for 2026: War, Farming & Trophy Strategies
Town Hall 13 introduces the Scattershot upgrade, the Royal Champion Hero, and one of the most aggressive attack metas in the game. The th13 base meta in 2026 is dominated by Super Witch attacks, Hybrid Queen-RC walks, and Electro Titan pushes. Defending against this requires a base that works on multiple levels simultaneously. This guide covers the full picture.

What Makes a Strong TH13 Base?
Core Design Principles
TH13 adds the Royal Champion — a Hero who throws her shield to hit defenses from range. Every base design at TH13 must account for the Royal Champion being used to snipe key defenses before the main attack begins. High-value single defenses on the outside of your base become immediate Royal Champion targets.
A strong th13 base in 2026 keeps all major defenses inside the second compartment ring minimum. Nothing important — no Scattershots, no Inferno Towers, no Eagle Artillery — should sit in the first ring where a Royal Champion can pick it off from outside the walls.
The three Scattershots at TH13 form the backbone of your base. Treat them like a defensive triangle: place them at equal distances from each other so their combined coverage reaches every corner of the map.
Defensive Building Placement
The Eagle Artillery reaches maximum power at TH13 — it targets any attacker who has deployed more than one hundred troops. Place it in the absolute center of your base. An Eagle Artillery in the core forces every attack to push all the way to the middle before it goes offline.
Five Air Defenses at TH13 enable true air-denial coverage. Place four in the corners and one in the center, creating overlapping coverage zones with no gaps. Any air attack that tries to path around the outside hits two Air Defenses simultaneously.

TH13 War Base Strategy: Stopping 3-Stars
Anti-Super Witch Setup
Super Witch attacks at TH13 are the most common three-star strategy in Clan War. Super Witches summon skeleton armies that clear compartments rapidly while the Witches themselves stay at the back dealing damage. The counter is extreme compartmentalization with Bomb Towers at every junction.
Place your Bomb Tower in the path of the most likely Super Witch push — typically along the longest wall section of your base. When it dies, its explosion hits the skeleton clusters and the Witches simultaneously, removing the protective front line instantly.
Use a Scattershot on ground-targeting mode on the side of the base where Super Witch attacks typically begin. The splash damage from a TH13-level Scattershot eliminates skeleton groups before they can clear walls for the main attack.
Anti-Electro Titan Defense
Electro Titans are high-hitpoint troops that deal chain lightning damage as they walk through your base. They're essentially walking Electro Dragons — and they're stopped the same way. Space your buildings to break the chain lightning, and place single-target Inferno Towers to burn through their hitpoints quickly.
A Tornado Trap placed in the core of your base stops an Electro Titan push cold. When triggered, it spins all nearby troops into a cluster where your Inferno Towers, Scattershots, and Eagle Artillery can eliminate them simultaneously.

TH13 Farming Base: Protecting Your Resources
Dark Elixir Protection at TH13
Hero upgrades at TH13 cost staggering amounts of Dark Elixir. Your Dark Elixir Storage needs to be the single most protected building in your entire base — more protected than the Town Hall itself in a farming context.
Surround it with your Eagle Artillery and one Inferno Tower on single-target mode. Add two Hidden Teslas adjacent to the storage. Any attacker who reaches this cluster faces four high-damage defenses simultaneously while the Eagle Artillery locks onto their highest-hitpoint troops from across the map.
Multi-Resource Management
Seven Storages of each type require strategic placement at TH13. Use an inner-outer split: three Storages per type in the outer ring as sacrificial resources, four per type in the core protected by real defenses. Most farming attacks won't bother pushing past the outer ring when they can fill their capacity on outer Storages alone.

5 Recommended TH13 Base Layout Styles
1. The Triple Scattershot Triangle
Three Scattershots placed in a true triangle formation with equal spacing. Their combined splash damage covers every tile of the base from at least one Scattershot. Eagle Artillery central. Both Inferno Towers flanking the Eagle Artillery. One of the strongest all-around war bases at TH13.
2. The Anti-Royal Champion Perimeter
No high-value defenses in the outer ring — every major defense sits at least two compartments deep. Royal Champion attacks find nothing worth targeting on the outside and are forced to commit the full army to reach anything meaningful. Consistently limits Royal Champion walk damage to zero.
3. The Eagle Artillery Fortress
The Eagle Artillery sits at the center of a five-compartment fortress. To reach it, attackers must breach five separate wall sections while facing Scattershot fire, Inferno Tower damage, and continuous Eagle Artillery targeting from the moment they deploy their hundredth troop. Most attacks fail to reach the core.
4. The Air Supremacy Base
Five Air Defenses in maximum spread, supplemented by three Scattershots on air-targeting mode and Seeking Air Mines at every Air Defense. Ground defenses are strong but secondary. This base destroys every air attack strategy at TH13 and forces opponents into ground attacks that face fresh trap placement.
5. The Hybrid Hero Protection Base
Both Scattershots and the Eagle Artillery form a core that also houses the Barbarian King and Archer Queen altars. Attackers who try to snipe Heroes from range face immediate Scattershot fire. A well-defended Hero altar forces attackers to commit to a much larger army to neutralize the Heroes before they can do defensive damage.

Common TH13 Base Mistakes to Avoid
Placing any Scattershot in the outer compartment ring is the most common TH13 base error. A Royal Champion plus three Earthquake Spells takes it down without triggering any interior defenses. Move all Scattershots at least two rings deep immediately.
Not accounting for the Super Witch meta is a mistake that costs three-stars in war consistently. If your base has long straight wall sections, Super Witch attacks walk through them in seconds. Break up every straight wall run with a compartment or a defense building.
Leaving the Tornado Trap unused or poorly placed wastes one of TH13's best defensive tools. It should sit at the most likely endpoint of any major push — where high-hitpoint troops compact together after breaching the second ring. A well-placed Tornado Trap turns a winning attack into a two-star instantly.
